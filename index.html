Step 2 — Configure Essential Settings

Payments

Go to Settings → Payments and activate Shopify Payments — zero extra transaction fees. You'll need your bank IBAN and a government ID. Also add PayPal.

Shipping

Start with free shipping on all orders. Build the cost into your product price. Free shipping consistently increases conversion rates by 15–25%.

⚠️ Common mistake: Skipping payment setup. Configure payments first — not after a customer tries to buy.

Step 5 — Add Your First Products

Import 3 to 5 products to start. Quality over quantity. A store with 5 well-presented products converts better than 100 poorly described ones.

Finding a winning product is 80% of the work in dropshipping. A great product sells itself. A poor one burns ad budget with nothing to show. Here's what actually works.

What Makes a Winning Product

  • Price sweet spot: $25–$80 — impulse-buy range with healthy margins
  • Not widely available in stores — if Amazon delivers it in 2 days, you can't compete
  • Solves a real problem or satisfies a strong emotional desire
  • Looks great in video — critical for Meta and TikTok ads
  • 60%+ gross margin — you need room to cover ad costs and still profit

Method 1 — Spy on Competitors' Ads

Free

Meta Ad Library

Search for stores in your niche. Visit their Facebook page → "See all ads." If an ad has been running 30+ days without stopping, the product is profitable.

Free

TikTok Creative Center

Browse Top Ads by industry and country. Filter by "E-commerce." The most-viewed ads reveal proven winning products.

Paid

Minea / AdSpy

Professional spy tools. Minea ($49/mo) searches Facebook, TikTok, and Pinterest simultaneously.

Meta Ads (Facebook + Instagram) remains the #1 traffic channel for dropshipping in 2026. Here's the exact structure beginners should use.

Campaign Structure for Testing

Level 1

1 Campaign — "Product Test"

Objective: Sales (Purchase). Campaign Budget Optimisation on. Total budget: $10–15/day.

Level 2

3–5 Ad Sets — different audiences

Each targets a different interest. The algorithm finds the best performer automatically.

Level 3

2–3 Ads per set — different creatives

Test video vs image, short vs long copy. Let data pick the winner.

✅ Never cut a campaign in the first 3 days. The Meta algorithm needs 50 purchase events to fully optimise. Give it time.

⚠️ Minimum budget to test properly: $50–$100 per product. Under $50, you won't have enough data to make a sound decision.

How to Scale a Shopify Dropshipping Store from $10 to $500/day

Advanced⏱ 14 min📅 May 2026

Not on Shopify yet?

Shopify is the only platform built to handle serious scale. 3 months free.

Start Free →

You have a product with a consistent ROAS above 2.5. Scaling incorrectly is one of the most expensive mistakes in dropshipping. Here's how to do it right.

The 2 Types of Scaling

Vertical Scaling — Increase Budgets

The golden rule: never increase budget by more than 20–30% per day. Bigger jumps reset the algorithm's learning phase and cause CPM to spike.

✅ Example: $10/day performing well → go to $12 → $15 → $19 → $24. Never jump from $10 to $50 in one day.

Horizontal Scaling — Duplicate Winners

Duplicate performing ad sets into new audiences. If "Yoga" converts, test "Pilates", "Home Workout", "Fitness Women 35–50". Expand reach without disrupting what works.

Pre-Scale Checklist

  • ROAS ≥ 2.5 stable for 7 consecutive days
  • At least 20–30 purchases tracked in Meta Events Manager
  • Enough cash flow to float ad costs ahead of revenue
  • Supplier can handle 3–5x current order volume
  • Customer service process in place

⚠️ Classic mistake: Scaling too early on thin data. An algorithm with only 5 purchases has insufficient signal. Patience is a genuine competitive advantage.
