Step 2 — Configure Essential Settings

Before adding products, take 20 minutes to set up your store correctly. These settings affect your ability to get paid and ship orders.

Payments

Go to Settings → Payments and activate Shopify Payments. This is Shopify's built-in payment processor — no extra transaction fees. You'll need your bank IBAN and a government ID. Also add PayPal — many customers trust it and won't buy without it.

Shipping

Start with a simple strategy: free shipping on all orders. Build the shipping cost into your product price. Free shipping consistently increases conversion rates by 15-25%.

⚠️ Common mistake: Skipping payment setup and trying to add products first. Set up payments before you have customers — not after.

Step 5 — Add Your First Products

Import 3 to 5 products maximum to start. Quality over quantity. A store with 5 perfectly presented products converts better than 100 poorly described ones.

Writing product descriptions that sell

  • Lead with benefits, not features. "Wake up pain-free every morning" beats "Memory foam pillow 60x40cm"
  • Address objections directly — fast delivery, easy returns, secure payment
  • Use social proof — "4,800+ happy customers" in the description

Pricing strategy

Multiply your supplier cost by 2.5 to 3x minimum. You need margin to absorb advertising costs and still profit. If the supplier price is $15, your selling price should be $37-$45.

⚠️ Biggest beginner mistake: Pricing too low out of fear. Low prices signal low quality. A $49 product often converts better than the same product at $19.

Step 6 — Launch and Test

Don't wait for perfection. Launch when your store looks professional — not when it looks perfect. Start with a small ad budget ($50-$100), gather data, and improve.

  • Create a Facebook Business Page and Instagram account for your store
  • Run a Meta Ads campaign at $10/day for 7-10 days
  • Monitor CPC, add-to-cart rate, and cost per purchase
  • If zero add-to-carts after $50 spent → change product or ad creative
  • If add-to-carts but no purchases → fix the checkout experience

Finding a winning product is 80% of the work in dropshipping. A great product sells itself. A mediocre one burns ad budget with nothing to show for it. Here's the system top dropshippers use.

What Makes a Winning Product

  • Price sweet spot: $25-$80 — impulse-buy range with healthy margins
  • Not available in stores — if Amazon has it for 2-day delivery, you can't compete
  • Solves a real problem or satisfies a strong emotional desire
  • Looks amazing in video — critical for Meta and TikTok ads
  • 60%+ gross margin — you need room to pay for ads and still profit
  • Lightweight — heavier products mean higher shipping costs that eat margins

Method 1 — Spy on Competitors' Ads

Free

Meta Ad Library

Search for stores in your niche on Facebook. Visit their page → click "See all ads." If an ad has been running for 30+ days without stopping, the product is profitable.

Free

TikTok Creative Center

Browse Top Ads by industry and country. Filter by "E-commerce" and sort by engagement. The most-viewed ads reveal proven winning products.

Paid

Minea / AdSpy

Professional spy tools. Minea ($49/mo) is the best — searches Facebook, TikTok, and Pinterest simultaneously. Worth every cent once you're serious.

Method 2 — Google Trends + Amazon

Google Trends tells you if a product's search volume is growing or declining. Always check before launching. Amazon Movers & Shakers shows what people are buying right now.

Meta Ads (Facebook + Instagram) remains the #1 traffic channel for dropshipping in 2026. Here's the exact campaign structure that beginners should use.

Campaign Structure for Testing

Level 1

1 campaign — "Product Test"

Objective: Sales (Purchase conversion). Campaign Budget Optimization on. Total budget: $10-15/day.

Level 2

3-5 ad sets — different audiences

Each ad set targets a different interest. Test simultaneously. The algorithm finds the best performer automatically.

Level 3

2-3 ads per set — different creatives

Test video vs image. Short vs long copy. Different hooks. Let data pick the winner.

The Golden Rules

✅ Never kill a campaign in the first 3 days. The Meta algorithm needs 50 purchase events to fully optimize. Give it time to learn.

⚠️ Minimum budget to test properly: $50-$100 per product test. Under $50, you won't have enough data to make a decision.

How to Scale a Shopify Store from $10 to $500/day Profit

Advanced⏱ 14 min📅 May 2026

Not on Shopify yet?

Shopify is the only platform built to handle serious scale. 3 months free.

Start Free →

You have a product with a consistent ROAS above 2.5. Now what? Scaling incorrectly is one of the most expensive mistakes in dropshipping. Here's how to do it right.

The 2 Types of Scaling

Vertical Scaling — Increase Budgets

The golden rule: never increase budget by more than 20-30% per day. Bigger jumps reset the algorithm's learning phase, causing CPM to spike and performance to drop.

✅ Example: $10/day performing well → go to $12 → $15 → $19 → $24. Never jump from $10 to $50 in one day.

Horizontal Scaling — Duplicate Winners

Duplicate performing ad sets into new audiences. If "Yoga" converts well, test "Pilates", "Home Workout", "Fitness Women 35-50". You're expanding reach without disrupting what works.

Before You Scale — Checklist

  • ROAS ≥ 2.5 stable for 7 consecutive days
  • At least 20-30 purchases tracked in Meta Events Manager
  • Enough cash flow (ad costs hit before revenue arrives)
  • Supplier can handle 3x-5x the current order volume
  • Customer service process in place (you'll have more issues at scale)

⚠️ Classic mistake: Scaling too early on thin data. An algorithm with only 5 purchases doesn't have enough signal. Patience is a competitive advantage.

Revenue Optimization Before Scaling

Before spending more on ads, maximize what you're already getting:

  • Activate abandoned cart emails (Klaviyo) — recovers 10-15% of lost sales
  • Add a post-purchase upsell (ReConvert) — increases AOV by 15%
  • A/B test your product page headline and main photo
  • Compress images — every second of load time costs conversions
  • Add 50+ verified reviews (Loox) — trust drives repeat purchases
